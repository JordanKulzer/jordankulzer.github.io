Privacy Policy

Pigment Pop for iOS · Effective October 16, 2026 · Contact: pigmentpop.support@gmail.com

Pigment Pop is a color-mixing puzzle game made by Jordan Kulzer. This page explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. The short version: the game itself keeps everything on your device and has no accounts; the only third party involved is Google AdMob, which shows the ads between games; the one purchase is handled by Apple.

What the game stores on your device

  • Your settings (sound, haptics, reduce motion), your bests and your run history, and the game in progress so it can be resumed. All of this stays on your iPhone (in the app's storage and iOS preferences). It is never sent to us, and deleting the app deletes it.
  • Whether you bought "Remove Ads", and the ad counters (how many runs you finished, when the last ad showed) that keep ads infrequent. Also only on the device.

There are no accounts, no sign-in, no leaderboards and no analytics of our own. The app never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a short full-screen ad now and then, only between games (never during one): none in your first three finished runs, at most one every third game over, and at least three minutes apart. The ads are served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC, which acts as a separate data controller for the data it collects.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app may collect:

  • Device and app identifiers: the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in the Apple prompt; otherwise app- or device-bounded identifiers Google uses for frequency capping and fraud prevention.
  • IP address, used to estimate a general (coarse) location, for example to serve ads in the right language and to apply the laws of your region.
  • Advertising data: which ads were shown, viewed or tapped, used to measure and improve ad performance.
  • Other interactions and diagnostics: app launch, ad loading performance and crash information related to the ad SDK, used to diagnose problems and keep the ads working.

Google explains how it uses this data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy. We receive only aggregate reports (impressions and earnings), never information about an individual player.

Your choices about ads

  • Apple's tracking prompt. Just before the first ad, iOS asks whether Pigment Pop may track you across apps. This is the IDFA permission. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you still see ads, but they are not personalized. You can change it any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent form (EU, UK and other regions where it applies). Google's consent form appears before the first ad and lets you accept or decline personalized ads. Where the law requires it, the app's Settings page shows an "Ad privacy options" row to change your answer later. Declining still shows ads, non-personalized ones.
  • Remove Ads. A one-time purchase in Settings turns ads off for good (see below). Once bought, the ad SDK is no longer asked for ads.
  • Google's own controls are at adssettings.google.com.

The "Remove Ads" purchase

"Remove Ads" is a single, non-consumable in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details; Apple's privacy policy covers the transaction. The app only stores a yes/no flag on your device and checks it against your App Store purchases when it starts, so a restore or a refund is reflected. "Restore purchases" in Settings re-reads that from Apple.

Children

Pigment Pop is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. The ad request is not tagged as child-directed.

Feedback emails

"Send feedback" in Settings opens your own Mail app with a message addressed to us. If you send it, we receive what you wrote and your email address, and we use them only to answer you and improve the game.

Changes

If the app starts collecting anything new (for example analytics or a leaderboard), this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version ships. The effective date above tells you when it last changed.

Contact

Questions or requests: pigmentpop.support@gmail.com.
