Pigment Pop

Mix colors. Pop targets. A puzzle game for iPhone.

Drag colored pieces onto the board. Red on yellow makes orange, yellow on blue makes green, blue on red makes purple. Match a target's color to pop it, chain pops into combos, and keep the board from filling up. Three modes: Standard, Marathon and Blitz. Free to play, with a short ad now and then between games; a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase turns them off.

Support

Found a bug, got stuck, or have an idea? Email pigmentpop.support@gmail.com. In the app, Settings → "Send feedback" opens a message with the build number filled in; Settings → Data → "Copy build info" and "Copy run log" give us what we need to look into a problem.

Purchases

"Remove Ads" is handled by Apple. If you reinstall or change phones, use Settings → "Restore purchases" in the app. Refunds go through Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

The game keeps your progress on your device and has no accounts. Ads are served by Google AdMob. Details and your choices: Privacy policy.
